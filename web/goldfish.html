Goldfish

    Goldfish: the deck played alone, no opponent and no card text (no draw, ramp or cost changes). Each turn it channels runes, draws, then plays the dearest cards it can pay for. Energy exhausts a rune, power recycles a rune of the card's domain. Rules and mulligan are in web/settings.js (goldfish).

    Card data via Piltover Archive. Source on GitHub.
